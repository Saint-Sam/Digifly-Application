# Connectome pictures

These five unmodified PNGs are the exact `logo` assets published in the public
neuPrint dataset catalogue, checked October 20, 2026:
https://neuprint.janelia.org/api/dbmeta/datasets

Credit: Janelia FlyEM and the collaborators named in each dataset description
(Cambridge Drosophila Connectomics Group and Google Connectomics as applicable).
neuPrintExplorer displays its content under CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The app scales each picture to fit its card while retaining its aspect ratio;
the original image bytes are unchanged. These are illustrations, not bundled
connectome skeletons or connectivity. The catalogue is a dated offline snapshot,
not a live availability or local-installation claim. Release identity remains
explicit and the downloader confirms the live server list before any acquisition.

- `connectome_male_cns.png`: `malecns-median-size-per-type-angle-view-white-cropped-small.png`
- `connectome_manc.png`: `manc-thumbnail-colors-adjusted-small.png`
- `connectome_hemibrain.png`: `hemibrain-icon-one-per-type-small.png`
- `connectome_optic_lobe.png`: `optic-lobe-logo-cropped.png`
- `connectome_mushroom_body.png`: `mushroombody-logo.png`

All originals use the prefix
`https://www.janelia.org/sites/default/files/Project%20Teams/Fly%20EM/`.
Male CNS v0.9/v1.0, all three MANC releases, and the two optic-lobe releases
share their respective family picture on neuPrint. They are not different local
libraries or permission to mix data between releases.
